About This Course

In today's digital economy, having strong digital skills is no longer optional—it's essential. Our Digital Skills course equips you with the modern tools and knowledge needed to thrive in the digital workplace, market your business online, and stay productive using the latest technology.

This course covers social media marketing, digital tools, online collaboration, and productivity strategies. Whether you are a business owner, job seeker, or professional, these skills will give you a competitive edge.

What You Will Learn

  • Social media marketing (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, TikTok)
  • Content creation: images, videos, and captions
  • Google Workspace: Docs, Sheets, Drive, Meet
  • Online collaboration and project management tools
  • Basic digital marketing and SEO concepts
  • Canva and graphic design basics
  • Productivity and time management with digital tools
  • Building a personal or business brand online

Who This Course Is For

  • Small business owners who want to market online
  • Job seekers wanting to add digital skills to their resume
  • Marketing professionals upgrading their skills
  • Content creators and influencers
  • Professionals needing to improve online productivity

Course Structure

Week 1

Social Media Marketing

Platform overview, content strategy, scheduling, and engagement tactics.

Week 2

Content Creation

Canva basics, photo editing, video editing, writing captions, and hashtags.

Week 3

Google Workspace & Tools

Google Docs, Sheets, Drive, Meet, and collaborative workflows.

Week 4

Digital Marketing Basics

SEO, email marketing, analytics, and online advertising concepts.

Week 5

Branding & Productivity

Personal branding, online presence, productivity tools, and automation.

Week 6

Projects & Campaigns

Create a real social media campaign, build a brand kit, and present results.

  • Content Creator
  • Virtual Assistant
  • Freelance Digital Marketer
