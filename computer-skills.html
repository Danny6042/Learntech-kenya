About This Course

Our Computer Skills course is a comprehensive program covering all essential areas of computing. From understanding hardware components to mastering software applications and basic networking, this course gives you the practical knowledge needed for today's digital workplace.

Whether you are preparing for an IT support role, want to upgrade your general computer knowledge, or need to understand how computers work from the ground up, this course is designed for you.

What You Will Learn

  • Computer hardware components and how they work
  • Operating systems (Windows, Linux basics)
  • Software installation and management
  • Basic networking and internet connectivity
  • Troubleshooting common computer problems
  • File management and data organization
  • Security basics and safe computing practices
  • Printer setup and peripheral devices

Who This Course Is For

  • Students preparing for IT or computer science studies
  • Office workers wanting to improve their technical confidence
  • Aspiring IT support technicians
  • Anyone who wants to understand computers beyond basic use
  • Small business owners managing their own IT

Course Structure

Week 1

Computer Hardware

CPU, RAM, storage, motherboard, peripherals, and how they connect.

Week 2

Operating Systems

Windows navigation, file management, settings, and user accounts.

Week 3

Software & Applications

Installing, updating, and uninstalling software. Understanding different software types.

Week 4

Networking & Internet

WiFi, routers, IP addresses, internet connectivity, and network troubleshooting.

Week 5

Security & Maintenance

Antivirus, backups, updates, password safety, and preventing common threats.

Week 6

Troubleshooting & Projects

Hands-on troubleshooting exercises and a final project to demonstrate skills.
