About This Course

Never used a computer before? This course is specifically designed for absolute beginners. We start from the very beginning and guide you through every essential skill you need to use a computer confidently in your daily life and work.

In a supportive and patient environment, you will learn how to use a keyboard and mouse, type efficiently, navigate the internet safely, manage your files, and use email. By the end of this course, you will have the digital confidence to pursue further training or use computers at work.

What You Will Learn

  • Computer basics: turning on/off, using mouse and keyboard
  • Touch typing and keyboard shortcuts
  • File management: creating, saving, moving, and deleting files
  • Internet browsing and safe searching
  • Email: creating, sending, and managing emails
  • Basic word processing and document creation
  • Online safety and avoiding scams

Who This Course Is For

  • Complete beginners who have never used a computer
  • Adults returning to education or the workforce
  • Seniors wanting to connect with family online
  • Students preparing for further computer studies
  • Anyone who wants to build digital confidence

Course Structure

Day 1-2

Computer Basics

Parts of a computer, turning on/off, using mouse and keyboard, typing practice.

Day 3-4

File Management

Creating folders, saving files, copying, moving, deleting, and organizing documents.

Day 5-6

Internet & Email

Opening a browser, searching, using email, creating a Gmail account, safety tips.

Day 7-8

Documents & Practice

Creating documents, formatting text, printing, and practice exercises.

Day 9-10

Review & Confidence

Review all skills, practice tests, and building digital confidence.
