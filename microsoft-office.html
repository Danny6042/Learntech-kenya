About This Course

Microsoft Office is the most widely used productivity suite in the world. Our Microsoft Office training course gives you mastery over Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook—skills that are essential in virtually every office and organization in Kenya and globally.

From creating professional documents to analyzing data with spreadsheets, designing impactful presentations, and managing email communication, this course covers everything you need to be productive in the modern workplace.

What You Will Learn

  • Microsoft Word: Formatting, tables, mail merge, templates, and long documents
  • Microsoft Excel: Formulas, functions, charts, pivot tables, and data analysis
  • Microsoft PowerPoint: Design, animations, transitions, and professional presentations
  • Microsoft Outlook: Email management, calendars, tasks, and contacts
  • Productivity shortcuts and time-saving techniques
  • Creating professional reports, budgets, and presentations
  • Collaboration features and sharing documents

Who This Course Is For

  • Job seekers who need office skills for employment
  • Office workers wanting to improve efficiency and productivity
  • Students preparing for academic projects and reports
  • Professionals looking to upgrade their skills
  • Anyone who wants to be more confident using office software

Course Structure

Week 1

Microsoft Word Essentials

Document creation, formatting, styles, tables, and mail merge.

Week 2

Microsoft Excel Basics

Data entry, formulas, functions, formatting, and basic charts.

Week 3

Advanced Excel

Pivot tables, data analysis, advanced formulas, and dashboards.

Week 4

Microsoft PowerPoint

Slide design, animations, transitions, and delivering presentations.
