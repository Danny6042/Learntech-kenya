About LearnTech Kenya

Our Mission

At LearnTech Kenya, we believe that technology skills should be accessible to everyone. Founded by Daniel Mutwiri, our mission is to bridge the digital skills gap in Kenya by providing practical, affordable, and high-quality computer training to individuals and businesses across the country.

We started with a simple vision: to create a learning environment where anyone, regardless of their background, can gain the digital skills needed to thrive in the modern economy. From coding and programming to Microsoft Office and digital marketing, we are committed to preparing our students for real-world success.

Why We Started

Too many talented Kenyans are held back by a lack of digital skills. Many training programs are too expensive, too theoretical, or too inaccessible. We wanted to change that. LearnTech Kenya was built on the belief that hands-on, practical training, delivered by experienced instructors, can transform lives and careers.

What We Offer

  • Practical, hands-on computer training courses
  • Small class sizes for personalized learning
  • Flexible schedules for working professionals
  • Both in-person and online training options
  • Recognized certificates upon completion
  • Career guidance and job readiness support
