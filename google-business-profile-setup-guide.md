## Step 5: Track Performance & Optimize

### View Your Insights
Go to your Google Business Profile dashboard and click **"Performance"**

Track these metrics:

**1. How customers find you:**
- **Direct searches:** People who searched your business name directly (brand awareness)
- **Discovery searches:** People who searched for a category, product, or service (new customers finding you)
- **Goal:** Increase discovery searches over time

**2. Where customers find you:**
- **Search views:** How many times your profile appeared in Google Search
- **Maps views:** How many times your profile appeared in Google Maps
- **Goal:** Increase both numbers

**3. Customer actions:**
- **Website clicks:** People clicking to your website
- **Direction requests:** People asking for directions to your location
- **Phone calls:** People clicking to call you
- **Message requests:** People sending messages
- **Goal:** Increase all actions, especially phone calls and website clicks

**4. Photo views:**
- How many times your photos were viewed
- Compare your photo views to similar businesses
- **Goal:** Upload more photos if you are below average

**5. Review trends:**
- Average star rating
- Total number of reviews
- Response rate (should be 100%)
- **Goal:** 4.5+ stars, 50+ reviews, 100% response rate

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Wrong business name:** Do not stuff keywords into your business name (e.g., "LearnTech Kenya - Best Computer Training School in Nairobi"). Google will penalize you. Use only your real business name.

2. **Ignoring reviews:** Not responding to reviews hurts your ranking and reputation. Respond to every single one.

3. **Inconsistent NAP:** Your Name, Address, and Phone must be identical on your website, GBP, social media, and every directory. Even small differences (e.g., "St." vs "Street") can hurt you.

4. **No photos:** Businesses with 10+ photos get 35% more clicks. Add photos regularly.

5. **No posts:** Inactive profiles rank lower. Post at least once per week.

6. **Fake reviews:** Never buy fake reviews or review yourself. Google detects this and can suspend your profile permanently.

7. **Wrong category:** Choosing "School" instead of "Computer Training School" will make you show up for the wrong searches.

8. **Not verifying:** Your profile will not show up until you verify. Do this immediately.

9. **One-time setup:** GBP is not a "set it and forget it" tool. Update it weekly for best results.

10. **Not using the review link:** Many students want to review but do not know how. Make it easy by sending them the direct link.
