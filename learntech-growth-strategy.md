## 3. SEO Strategy

### On-Page SEO
- **Target Keywords:** computer training Nairobi, coding classes Kenya, Microsoft Office training Nairobi, basic computer training Kenya, digital skills course Nairobi, tech education Kenya, programming classes Nairobi, Excel training Kenya, computer school Nairobi, learn coding Kenya
- **Title Tags:** Every page has unique, keyword-rich titles (50-60 characters)
- **Meta Descriptions:** Compelling descriptions with keywords and calls-to-action (150-160 characters)
- **Header Tags:** Proper H1, H2, H3 hierarchy
- **Schema Markup:** LocalBusiness schema, Course schema, Review schema
- **Internal Linking:** Cross-link all course pages, blog, about, and contact pages
- **Image Alt Text:** Descriptive alt text for all images (add when images are uploaded)
- **Canonical Tags:** Set on all pages to avoid duplicate content issues
- **Page Speed:** Optimize images, minify CSS/JS, enable compression

### Off-Page SEO
- **Google Business Profile:** Create and verify (detailed steps below)
- **Backlink Building:**
  - Guest post on Kenyan tech blogs (Techweez, SokoN, Nairobi Tech Week)
  - Get listed in Kenyan business directories (Yellow Pages Kenya, Kenya Business Listings)
  - Partner with local businesses for mutual links
  - Sponsor local tech events and get mentioned
- **Social Signals:** Share blog posts and course updates on social media
- **Online Reviews:** Encourage students to leave Google Reviews

### Technical SEO
- Submit sitemap.xml to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools
- Set up robots.txt
- Ensure mobile-friendly design (responsive CSS implemented)
- Fix broken links
- Add SSL certificate (GitHub Pages provides this automatically)
- Implement breadcrumb navigation

## 7. Paid Advertising Strategy

### Google Ads (Budget: 5,000-15,000 KES/month)
- **Search Ads:** Target keywords like "computer training Nairobi," "coding classes Kenya," "Excel training Nairobi"
- **Ad Copy:** Include price, location, and call-to-action
- **Landing Pages:** Use course-specific pages
- **Call Extensions:** Add phone number
- **Location Targeting:** Nairobi, Kiambu, surrounding areas

### Facebook/Instagram Ads (Budget: 5,000-10,000 KES/month)
- **Audience:** Nairobi, age 18-35, interested in education, technology, career development
- **Ad Types:** Carousel (showcase courses), Video (student testimonials), Lead Gen (collect leads)
- **Retargeting:** Website visitors who didn't enroll
- **Lookalike Audiences:** Based on past students

### WhatsApp Click-to-Message Ads
- Run ads that open WhatsApp chat directly
- Fastest way to convert interested leads
- Use for course inquiries and enrollment
